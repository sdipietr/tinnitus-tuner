Before you start

Headphones. Playback starts silent. Keep volume low. Stop if it gets worse.

Not a treatment. Not medical advice.

Find the pitch (Match)

Press Play, then sweep until the tone matches the ring. Use ±1 / ±10 to fine-tune.

Sine is a pure tone. Narrow noise is a thin hiss at that pitch.

Notch

Once the pitch is found, switch to Notch. A bed of sound should remain, with a hole at that frequency. The ring sits in the hole.

If the noise vanishes, volume is too low or the notch is too wide.

Mask / residual

A short louder burst, then silence. A home trick, not a cure.

Ear

Pan toward the ear that rings.

Settings

Settings stay on this device (browser storage). Spacebar toggles play.
